## Azure Architecture Lens

When evaluating any Azure solution, think in layers:

- Identity: Entra ID, RBAC, Managed Identity
- Network: VNet, Subnet, NSG, DNS, Load Balancing
- Compute: VMs, App Service, Functions, Containers
- Data: Storage Accounts, SQL, Cosmos DB
- Security: Key Vault, Defender for Cloud, policy controls
- Operations: Monitor, Log Analytics, backup and recovery
- Governance: Policy, tags, management groups, landing zone guardrails
- Cost: Budgets, cost analysis, right-sizing, reservations
- Reliability: Availability zones, DR patterns, backup, resilience testing

Build this habit early and your design quality will improve fast.
