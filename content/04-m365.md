## 🧪 Microsoft 365 Trial Tenant Options
Use a trial tenant before you start hands-on admin and certification work.

- Trial tenants are dedicated environments with a unique `.onmicrosoft.com` domain and admin center access.
- Business trial: 30-day free trial for Business Basic, Business Standard, Business Premium, Microsoft 365 Apps for business, and Defender for Business. A credit/debit card is required; billing only starts after the trial ends.
- Developer Program sandbox: a renewable Microsoft 365 E5 developer tenant (typically 90 days) that is ideal for ongoing training, tenant admin practice, and lab exercises.
- Trial tenants include Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and core tenant management features.
- Manage trial subscriptions through the Microsoft 365 Admin Center: assign licenses, create users, control self-service trial settings, and cancel before billing if needed.
